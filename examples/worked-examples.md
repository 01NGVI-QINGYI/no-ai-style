# Editing decisions on fictional material

These examples are invented for instruction. Each revision uses only
information already present in its input.

## English: expose the procedure

**Input:** For clarity, it should be mentioned that the instrument was
calibrated before each of the six sessions.

**Revision:** The instrument was calibrated before each of the six sessions.

The edit removes the announcement of a statement. It keeps the procedure,
its timing, and the number of sessions. It adds no calibration standard.

## English: retain an inconvenient result

**Input:** Unfortunately, three of the 18 files could not be opened.

**Revision:** Three of the 18 files could not be opened.

The evaluation disappears; the failed files remain. Do not invent a
corruption diagnosis or claim that the other files were analyzed.

## 中文：缩短重复说明

**输入：** 本次档案检索的时间范围为四月，不包括四月以外的其他月份。

**修改：** 本次档案检索仅覆盖四月。

月份和排除范围保留。不能补充年份、档案数量或选择四月的理由。

## 日本語：保留观察边界

**入力：** 今回の観測では、8 °C未満でセンサーの信号は記録されなかった。

**判断：** そのまま残す。

否定表現は観測結果を伝えている。「センサーは8 °C以上で動作する」
への変更は、動作条件について別の主張を加えるため採用しない。

## File review: suggestions are separate from edits

**Request:** Audit this paragraph. Do not rewrite it.

**Delivery:** Identify the passage, describe the reading problem, and
suggest an operation. Leave the source file unchanged. When evidence is
missing, name what is needed rather than supplying a plausible detail.
