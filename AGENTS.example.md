# Project-specific writing convention (optional)

When asked to edit or audit academic or professional writing, use
`no-ai-style` from `.agents/skills/no-ai-style/SKILL.md`, when available.

Follow the user's authorized scope. Preserve claims, numbers, citations,
methods, limitations, and disclosure requirements. Do not modify files
in `audit` mode.

Do not force this writing skill onto unrelated coding or research tasks.
